Taxonomy — Classification की Science

🔬 Taxonomy क्या है?

Kingdom Monera

🦠 Kingdom Monera — Key Features

  • Cell type: Prokaryotic (no true nucleus, no membrane-bound organelles)
  • Organization: Unicellular, microscopic
  • Cell Wall: Peptidoglycan (bacteria में)
  • Nutrition: Autotrophic (photosynthetic/chemosynthetic) या Heterotrophic (saprophytic/parasitic)
  • Example: Cyanobacteria (blue-green algae — photosynthetic), Eubacteria

✅ Eubacteria (True Bacteria)

  • Most common group
  • Cell wall में Peptidoglycan होता है
  • Normal environments में रहते हैं

🌋 Archaebacteria (Ancient Bacteria)

  • Extreme environments: Hot springs, Salt lakes, Swamps
  • Cell wall में Peptidoglycan absent
4

Kingdom Protista

🔭 Kingdom Protista — Key Features

  • Cell type: Eukaryotic (true nucleus + organelles) — cell wall absent
  • Organization: Mostly unicellular, कुछ colonial या simple multicellular
  • Locomotion: Appendages से (cilia, flagella, pseudopodia)
  • Nutrition: Autotrophic (Algae) या Heterotrophic (Protozoans)

Kingdom Fungi

🍄 Kingdom Fungi — Key Features

  • Cell type: Eukaryotic
  • Organization: Mostly multicellular (कुछ unicellular — जैसे Yeast)
  • Cell Wall: Chitin से बनी (cellulose नहीं!)
  • Nutrition: Heterotrophic, Saprophytic (dead matter खाते हैं), कुछ parasitic/mutualistic
  • Study of Fungi: Mycology
  • Study of Algae: Phycology (Algology)
🤝 Lichen = Fungi + Algae का Symbiotic Relationship (साथ रहना)
Fungus → Shelter, Protection, Water और Nutrients देता है
Blue-green algae (या Green algae) → Photosynthesis से Fungus को food बनाकर देता है
Lichen = Natural Indicator भी है!

Kingdom Plantae — Overview

🌱 Kingdom Plantae — Basic Features

  • Cell type: Eukaryotic
  • Organization: Multicellular
  • Cell Wall: Cellulose से बनी
  • Nutrition: Autotrophic (Chlorophyll से Photosynthesis)

📏 Classification का Basis

  • Plant body और distinct components का differentiation
  • Vascular tissues की presence या absence
  • Seeds bear करने की ability
  • Seeds fruits के अंदर enclosed हैं या नहीं
  • Flowers produce करने की ability

1. Thallophyta (Algae)

🌊 Thallophyta — Key Features

  • Cell type: Eukaryotic
  • Structure: Simple — no differentiated roots/stems (Thallus होता है)
  • Vascularity: Non-vascular (No xylem, No phloem)
  • Habitat: Mostly aquatic (freshwater/marine)
  • Movement: Non-motile
  • Nutrition: Autotrophic, photosynthetic (chlorophyll)
  • Study: Phycology (Algology)
  • Algae cell wall = outer layer of Cellulose + inner layer of Pectin

2. Bryophyta (Amphibians of Plant Kingdom)

🌿 Bryophyta — Key Features

  • Plant Kingdom के "Amphibians" — Land और Water दोनों में exist करते हैं
  • Vascularity: Non-vascular (No xylem, No phloem)
  • Nutrition: Autotrophic
  • Structure: Well-differentiated body लेकिन true roots/stems/leaves absent
  • Root-like structures = Rhizoids
  • Reproduction: Spores से (seeds नहीं) — water में
  • Male part: Antheridium | Female part: Archegonium
  • Habitat: Damp और moist areas

🌱 Mosses

Examples: Funaria

Damp walls और मिट्टी पर उगते हैं

🍃 Liverworts

Examples: Marchantia, Riccia

Flat, liver-shaped body

Horn-like sporophyte होता है

3. Pteridophyta (First Vascular Plants)

🌿 Pteridophyta — Key Features

  • पहले terrestrial vascular plants — Xylem और Phloem दोनों present
  • Well-differentiated roots, stems और leaves (Ferns में leaves = Fronds)
  • Leaves के leaflets जो spores bear करते हैं = Sporophylls
  • Hidden reproductive organs → Cryptogams में आते हैं
  • Habitat: Rocks की cracks, moist और shady places
  • Examples: Marsilea, Ferns, Horsetails, Selaginella, Adiantum
10

4. Gymnosperms (Naked Seeds)

🌲 Gymnosperms — Key Features

  • Meaning: Gymno (without cover) + Sperma (seeds) = Naked seeds
  • Vascularity: Vascular plants — True roots, stem, leaves (xylem + phloem)
  • ⚠️ Note: Phloem Companion Cells और Xylem Vessels absent
  • Seeds: Produce करते हैं लेकिन seeds naked हैं — fruit में enclosed नहीं
  • Characteristics: Perennials, Evergreen, Mostly woody
  • Examples: Pine, Deodar (देवदार), Cycas
  • Phanerogamae में आते हैं
11

5. Angiosperms (Covered Seeds — Flowering Plants)

🌸 Angiosperms — Key Features

  • Meaning: Angio (covered) + Sperma (seeds) = Seeds fruit में enclosed
  • Characteristics: Flowering plants — seeds fruit के अंदर बंद
  • Vascularity: Fully vascular (Xylem + Phloem) — Highly evolved
  • Reproduction: Flowers = reproductive organs
  • Flower → Fruit: Ovary → Fruit बनता है | Ovules → Seeds बनते हैं
