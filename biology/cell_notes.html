3. Cell Theory

1

Schleiden & Schwann (1838)

Sabhi living organisms cells se bane hain, aur cell life ki basic unit hai.

2

Rudolf Virchow (1855) — "Omnis cellula e cellula"

Matlab: naye cells hamesha purane cells se bante hain. Naye cells suddenly nahi aate!

  • Ye semi-living hote hain — reproduce karne ke liye host cell chahiye.

5. Cell Membrane

Kya hoti hai?

  • Ise Plasma Membrane bhi kehte hain.
  • Thin, elastic aur flexible structure hoti hai.
  • Animal cells mein outermost covering, plant cells mein cell wall ke andar hoti hai.

Composition (Kya bani hoti hai?)

Proteins 50% Lipids 50%

Lipids ke 2 types: Phospholipids Cholesterol

Lipids = organic molecules jo water mein dissolve nahi hote par non-polar solvents mein hote hain (fats & waxes).

Functions (Kya kaam karti hai?)

  • 🛡️ Protective barrier — bodyguard ki tarah
  • 📐 Cell ko shape deti hai
  • 📦 Internal contents ko protect karti hai
  • 🚪 Substances ki movement control karti hai — Selectively Permeable Membrane

6. Movement Across Cell Membrane

💨

Diffusion

No Energy Needed
  • High → Low concentration movement
  • Rate: Gases > Liquids > Solids
  • Example: Agarbatti ki khushbu
💧

Osmosis

Water Movement
  • Water ka movement semi-permeable membrane se
  • High → Low concentration
  • No energy, equilibrium tak chalta hai
⚡

Active Transport

Energy Required
  • Low → High concentration
  • Reverse Osmosis bhi kehte hain
  • Cellular energy (ATP) chahiye
Solution Type Description Effect on Cell Solute vs Solvent
Isotonic Normal solution No change in size Solute = Solvent
Hypotonic Dilute solution Cell swells (Turgidity) Solute < Solvent
Hypertonic Concentrated solution Cell shrinks (Plasmolysis) Solute > Solvent
Turgor Pressure: Hypotonic solution mein cell mein pani ghusta hai → cell phool jata hai → Turgor pressure badhta hai → Cells ka growth aur enlargement hota hai.

8. Cytoplasm

Kya hota hai?

  • Saare cell organelles cytoplasm mein hi hote hain.
  • Cytosol: Water-based fluid portion (aqueous part).
  • Cytoplasmic Inclusions: Non-living components — pigments, nutrients etc.

9. Nucleus & Nucleoplasm

Nucleus — "Brain of the Cell"

  • Discovery: Robert Brown, 1831
  • Cell ke center mein hota hai.
  • Eukaryotic cell ka sabse bada organelle hai (double-membraned).
  • Double-layered nuclear membrane nucleus ko cytoplasm se alag karti hai.
  • Saare cell organelles ki activities control karta hai.
Protoplasm = Cell membrane + Cytoplasm + Nucleus — ye cell ka living material hai. Isme nucleic acids, proteins, lipids aur carbohydrates hote hain.

Nucleoplasm

  • Purkinje ne 1839 mein term diya.
  • Thick fluid se bana hota hai — chromatin fibers (DNA + proteins) hote hain.
  • Nucleolus: Non-membrane-bound structure — ribosome biogenesis (banane) ke liye.
  • Chromatin material: DNA + Proteins (mainly Histones) ka complex.

Euchromatin

Loosely packed — active (transcription hoti hai)

Heterochromatin

Tightly packed — inactive (transcription nahi hoti)

10. Chromosomes & DNA

Chromosomes

  • Thread-like structures jo nucleus mein hote hain — DNA aur genes carry karte hain.
  • Genetic information ek cell se doosre mein le jaate hain.
  • Components: DNA + Proteins (Histones)
  • Human mein total: 46 chromosomes = 23 pairs
  • 22 pairs = Autosomal  |  1 pair = Sex (XX female, XY male)

DNA

  • Genetic information carry karta hai.
  • Genes: DNA ke functional segments — next generation ko info transfer karte hain.
  • Discovery: Friedrich Miescher (1869)
  • Double-Helical Structure: Watson and Crick (1953)
Nucleotide = Phosphate + Deoxyribose + Nitrogenous Base
Base Pairs: Guanine ↔ Cytosine  |  Adenine ↔ Thymine (DNA)
RNA mein: Adenine ↔ Uracil (Thymine ki jagah)

12. Cell Organelles

⚡

Mitochondria

Powerhouse of the Cell
  • Discovery: Kolliker (1857)
  • Size: 1–2 µm, Sausage shape
  • ATP (Adenosine Triphosphate) synthesis karta hai — Energy currency
  • Apoptosis (programmed cell death) mein role
  • Apna DNA aur ribosomes hote hain
  • 2 membranes: Outer (porous) + Inner (ATP synthesis)
🏭

Ribosomes

Protein Factory
  • Discovery: George E. Palade (1955)
  • No membrane hoti in mein
  • Protein synthesis ke liye essential
  • Both prokaryotes & eukaryotes mein hote hain
🚇

Endoplasmic Reticulum

Transport System
  • Sac-like network of structures
  • Plasma membrane ↔ Nuclear membrane se connected
  • RER: Ribosomes present → Protein synthesis
  • SER: No ribosomes → Lipid/fat synthesis + Detoxification (liver mein cytochrome P450)
📬

Golgi Bodies

Post Office of Cell
  • Discovery: Camillo Golgi (1898)
  • Vesicles, cisternae, vacuoles se bana
  • Cis face: Convex, nucleus ki taraf (receiving)
  • Trans face: Concave, plasma membrane ki taraf (shipping)
  • Proteins & lipids packaging + dispatch
  • Simple sugars → Complex sugars banata hai
  • Lysosomes banata hai
💣

Lysosomes

Suicide Bag of Cell
  • Digestive enzymes (made in RER) se filled
  • Cell ka waste disposal system
  • Cell damage hone par burst hote hain → apni hi cell digest karte hain
🫙

Vacuoles

Storage Bags
  • Membrane-bound, solid/liquid filled sac
  • Plants: Large (50–90% cell volume), permanent, cell sap store karte hain, turgidity dete hain
  • Animals: Small, numerous, temporary, glucose/glycogen/protein store karte hain
  • Waste products temporarily stored in vacuoles
🔋

Peroxisomes

Detox Center
  • Very long chain fatty acids ko break karta hai (beta oxidation)
  • Catalase enzyme hoti hai → Harmful H₂O₂ → Water + O₂
  • Free radical scavengers — cell ko detoxify karte hain

13. Plastids

Plastids sirf plant cells mein hote hain, animal cells mein nahi. Inke paas apna DNA aur ribosomes hote hain.
🌱

Chloroplasts

Photosynthesis
  • Green plastids — chlorophyll pigment hota hai
  • Yellow/orange pigments: Xanthophyll, Carotenes
  • Chlorophyll mein Magnesium (Mg²⁺) hota hai — solar energy capture ke liye
🍅

Chromoplasts

Color Provider
  • Different pigments → color provide karte hain
  • Tomato red → Lycopene
  • Chilli red → Capsanthin
  • Anthocyanin → fruits/veges ka color
  • Yellow color → Xanthophyll
⬜

Leucoplasts

Storage (Colorless)
  • Amyloplast → Carbohydrates store
  • Aleuroplast → Proteins store
  • Elaioplast → Fats store

14. Cell Division

Cell division = ek parent cell 2 ya zyada daughter cells mein divide hoti hai.

Division ki Zaroorat Kyun?

  • 1️⃣ Organism ka growth
  • 2️⃣ Old, dead, injured cells ko replace karna
  • 3️⃣ Gamete formation (Meiosis se)
Feature Mitosis Meiosis
CellsSomatic (body) cellsGerm (reproductive) cells
Nucleus dividesOnceTwice
Daughter cells24
Chromosome numberDiploid (2n) — same as parentHaploid (n) — half of parent
Genetic identityIdentical to parentDifferent from parent
PurposeGrowth, repair, asexual reproductionGamete formation (egg & sperm)
Genetic variationNoYes (crossing over)
Malfunction leads toCancer (uncontrolled division)Down's, Klinefelter's, Turner's syndrome

Mitosis ke Phases (PMAT)

  • Prophase: Chromosomes condense aur visible ho jaate hain
  • Metaphase: Chromosomes cell ke equator pe align ho jaate hain
  • Anaphase: Sister chromatids separate ho jaate hain, opposite poles pe jaate hain
  • Telophase: 2 new nuclei form hote hain

15. Cell Cycle

Interphase (I phase)

3 sub-phases hoti hain:

  • G1 Phase (Gap 1): Cell grow karta hai, normal functions karta hai, DNA replication ki preparation
  • S Phase (Synthesis): DNA replicate hota hai — har chromosome ki 2 identical copies ban jaati hain
  • G2 Phase (Gap 2): Cell aur grow karta hai, ensure karta hai ki DNA replication complete ho gayi, mitosis ke liye ready hota hai

M Phase (Mitotic Phase)

Cell ka nucleus aur cytoplasm divide hote hain.

  • Karyokinesis: Nucleus ka division (Prophase → Metaphase → Anaphase → Telophase)
  • Cytokinesis: Cytoplasm ka division

16. DNA Replication

DNA Replication = Cell apna exact DNA copy banata hai cell division se pehle — taaki har naye cell ko same genetic information mile.

Semi-Conservative Replication

2 new double-stranded DNA molecules bante hain — har ek mein ek original strand aur ek naya strand hota hai.

Enzymes (DNA Replication ke liye)

Topoisomerase

DNA Primase
Replication initiate karta hai (new strands produce)
DNA Polymerase
DNA ki amount double karta hai (Builder 🏗️)
DNA Ligase
DNA strands ko join karta hai (Gluer 🔗)

17. Endocytosis vs Exocytosis

🍽️ Endocytosis — Cell Eating/Drinking

  • Active transport ka ek type
  • Cell external materials ko engulf karta hai → vesicle banata hai andar laane ke liye
  • Phagocytosis: Cell eating
  • Pinocytosis: Cell drinking
  • Example: Amoeba food particles trap karta hai

🤮 Exocytosis — Cell Vomiting

  • Cell molecules ko bahar transport karta hai
  • Vesicles cell membrane se fuse hote hain → contents extracellular space mein release hote hain
  • Vomited particles = undigested residues from endocytosis → enzymes & hormones

Amoeba Digestion Steps

18. Cytoskeleton & Centrosome

Cytoskeleton

  • Cell ko structural support deta hai.
  • Cell movement mein help karta hai.

Centrosome — MTOC

  • Microtubule Organizing Center — cell division aur structure regulate karta hai.
  • Microtubules organize karta hai — cell structure, movement aur division ke liye essential.
  • Har centriole ek cylindrical structure hai jो microtubules se bana hai — 9+0 arrangement.

Microtubules

Hollow, cylindrical structures — tubulin proteins se bane hain. Cell ka cytoskeleton form karte hain, structural support, movement aur cell division mein role.
