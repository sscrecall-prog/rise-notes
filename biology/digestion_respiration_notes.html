2. Mouth & Oesophagus

👄 Mouth (Oral / Buccal Cavity)

  • Digestion yahan se start hoti hai.
  • Teeth → food chew karte hain (mechanical digestion).
  • Saliva (salivary glands se) → Salivary Amylase contain karta hai → starch (complex carbs) → simple carbs.
  • Tongue food mix karne aur swallowing mein help karta hai.

🔗 Pharynx & Oesophagus

  • Pharynx: Mouth ko oesophagus se connect karta hai.
  • Oesophagus: Length = 25 cm — muscular tube jo food ko stomach tak push karta hai peristalsis (wave-like movement) se.

3. Stomach

Stomach — J-shaped muscular organ

Gastric Juice pH: 1.6
  • HCl (Hydrochloric Acid): Germs kill karta hai, acidic medium provide karta hai. Gastric juice mein present (pH 1.6).
  • Pepsin: Proteins digest karta hai — sirf acidic medium mein kaam karta hai.
  • Mucus: Delicate stomach lining ko acid se protect karta hai.
  • Sphincter Muscle: Food ko stomach se small intestine mein pass karne deta hai.

4. Small Intestine

Small Intestine — Longest part of alimentary canal

Length: 6.5 to 7.5 meters
  • Main site of digestion and absorption.
  • 3 parts mein divided: Duodenum Jejunum Ileum
  • Yahan liver se bile aur pancreas se pancreatic juice receive hoti hai.

5. Bile & Pancreas

💛 Bile (Bile Juice)

Liver se, Gall Bladder mein stored
  • Bile ek enzyme nahi — supporting role play karta hai.
  • Alkaline (basic) in nature.
  • Acidic food ko neutralize karta hai small intestine se pehle.
  • Pancreatic + intestinal enzymes ke liye favorable pH create karta hai.
  • Emulsification of Fats: Large fat globules → small globules.

🟡 Pancreas

Pancreatic Juice release karta hai
  • Function: Carbs, proteins aur fats digest karta hai.
  • Amylase: Carbs break down.
  • Lipase: Fats → fatty acids + glycerol.
  • Trypsin: Basic medium mein kaam karta hai → Proteins → amino acids.
Pepsin vs Trypsin: Pepsin = Acidic medium (Stomach) | Trypsin = Basic medium (Small Intestine) — dono protein digest karte hain!

6. Large Intestine

Large Intestine — Length: 1.5 m

  • Function: Remaining undigested food se water + electrolytes absorb karna → feces solidify → elimination.
  • Cecum: Large intestine ka pehla part — small intestine ko colon se connect karta hai.
  • Colon: Indigestible food residues se water absorb karna.

7. Liver

Liver — Human body ki sabse badi gland

Hepatology = Study of Liver | Dark red-brown, right side of abdomen
  • Glucose → Glycogen (storage) — process = Glycogenesis.
  • Plasma proteins synthesize karta hai.
  • Amino acid metabolism (deamination + urea formation).
  • Blood sugar levels maintain karne ke liye glycogen store karta hai.

Kupffer Cells

  • Star-shaped phagocytic cells — liver sinusoids ki lining mein.
  • Old/damaged RBCs break down karte hain.
  • Blood se pathogens + debris clean karte hain.
  • Regenerative ability hoti hai.

Hepatocytes

  • Liver ki main parenchymal (functional) cells.
  • Carbs, proteins aur fats ka metabolism.
  • Blood detoxification.

10. Respiration in Plants

Respiration: Biochemical process — organic compounds ka oxidative breakdown → energy ATP (Adenosine Triphosphate) ke form mein release.
Breathing: Oxygen inhale karna aur Carbon Dioxide exhale karna (lungs ke through).

🌱 By Roots

  • Air soil particles ke beech spaces mein hoti hai (soil interspaces).
  • Root hairs + epiblema cells (outermost layer of young roots) soil air ke direct contact mein.
  • O₂ diffuse hoti hai root hairs → inner tissues → aerobic respiration → glucose break down → energy.

🍃 By Leaves

  • Leaves + young stems — thin structure + pores → quick gas exchange.
  • Mature stems → impermeable epidermis → water loss prevent.
  • Epidermis mein stomata (tiny openings) hote hain.
  • Har stoma ke 2 guard cells hote hain → opening/closing control.
  • Gas exchange + transpiration regulate karte hain.
📌 Guttation

Plants mein raat ko water loss — hydathodes (leaf edges pe present) ke through — water droplets ki form mein.

12. Nose → Pharynx → Larynx → Trachea → Bronchi → Bronchioles → Alveoli

👃 Nose & Nasal Cavity

First entry point for air
  • Nose ke andar hair → dust + impurities trap karte hain.
  • Mucus lining → air moisten karta hai, dust + bacteria trap karta hai, respiratory tract protect karta hai.

🗣️ Pharynx

  • Air aur food dono ke liye common passage.
  • Nasal cavity ko larynx se connect karta hai.
  • Air ko trachea ki taraf direct karta hai.

🎤 Larynx (Voice Box)

Adam's Apple bhi kehte hain
  • Vocal cords contain karta hai — air pass hone par sound produce hoti hai.
  • Trachea ke top pe located hai.

🌬️ Trachea (Windpipe)

  • Long tube — larynx se bronchi tak air carry karta hai.
  • C-shaped cartilage rings — trachea ko collapse hone se rokते hain, airway open rakhte hain.

🌿 Bronchi

  • Trachea 2 bronchi mein divide hoti hai — ek har lung ke liye.
  • Har bronchus lung mein enter karta hai, air deeper carry karta hai.
  • Mucus + cilia — dust aur microbes trap karte hain.

🌱 Bronchioles

  • Lungs ke andar har bronchus aur chhoti tubes mein branch karta hai = bronchioles.
  • Narrower + more numerous — lungs mein air spread karte hain.
  • Directly alveoli (air sacs) mein lead karte hain.

🫧 Alveoli

Gas Exchange Site
  • Tiny balloon-like structures — bronchioles ke ends pe.
  • Lungs mein millions of alveoli — capillaries (tiny blood vessels) se surrounded.
  • Function: O₂ air se blood mein diffuse hoti hai → CO₂ blood se alveoli mein → exhale.
  • Thin walls + large surface area → gas exchange bahut efficient.
Diaphragm: Chest cavity ka floor banata hai. | Respiration Centre: Medulla Oblongata mein hota hai.

15. Aerobic vs Anaerobic Respiration

BasisAerobic RespirationAnaerobic Respiration
OxygenO₂ requiredO₂ NOT required
SiteMitochondria + CytoplasmCytoplasm only
End ProductsCO₂ + H₂O + EnergyCO₂ + C₂H₅OH (ethanol) + Energy
Energy ReleasedLarge amountSmall amount
ATP Produced38 ATPOnly 2 ATP
ExampleHumans, most animalsYeast (dough), Muscle cells (lack of O₂)
🍞 Yeast in Dough

Yeast anaerobic respiration karta hai → CO₂ release → dough soft aur fluffy banta hai (baked goods mein use).

💪 Muscle Cramps

Muscles mein O₂ ki kami → Anaerobic respiration → Lactic acid accumulate hota hai → Cramps.

Glycolysis pathway:
Glucose (C₆H₁₂O₆) → Pyruvate (3C) + 2 ATP
In Yeast (no O₂): Pyruvate → Ethanol + CO₂ + Energy
In Muscles (no O₂): Pyruvate → Lactic Acid + Energy
In Mitochondria (O₂ present): Pyruvate → CO₂ + H₂O + Energy (Aerobic)

16. Cellular Respiration

Cellular Respiration kya hai?

Process jisme cells glucose (sugar) break down karte hain — O₂ ki presence mein — energy ATP (Adenosine Triphosphate) ke form mein release karte hain.

Formula: C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂ → 6CO₂ + 6H₂O + Energy (ATP & heat)

17. Steps in Cellular Respiration

1

Glycolysis — Cytoplasm mein

Glucose (6C) → 2 molecules of Pyruvate (3C each). Small amount of ATP + NADH produce hote hain.

2

Krebs Cycle (Citric Acid Cycle) — Mitochondria mein

Pyruvate further break down hota hai. CO₂ release hoti hai. Energy carriers NADH + FADH₂ produce hote hain.

3

Electron Transport Chain (ETC) — Inner Mitochondrial Membrane mein

NADH + FADH₂ electrons donate karte hain. O₂ = final electron acceptor. Up to 34 ATP produce hota hai. Total ~38 ATP per glucose aerobic respiration mein.

NADH (Nicotinamide Adenine Dinucleotide): ek coenzyme hai jo cellular respiration mein electrons ko Electron Transport Chain tak transport karta hai — vital role.
