1. Introduction to Reproduction

Reproduction kya hai?

Reproduction woh biological process hai jisme living organisms offspring produce karte hain — species ko continue karne ke liye. Ye individual survival ke liye zaruri nahi, but species ke survival ke liye crucial hai.

FeatureAsexual ReproductionSexual Reproduction
Parents involvedOnly one parentBoth male & female
Occurs inUnisexual / lower plantsBisexual / higher plants
GeneticsGenetic similarityGenetic diversity (higher survival chance)
Reproductive organsNot presentFully developed parts present
Metagenesis: Ek aisa life cycle jisme ek generation asexually reproduce kari, doosri sexually. Kuch diploid kuch haploid hote hain. E.g. Obelia

2. Types of Gametic Fusion

Isogamy

Morphologically similar gametes ka fusion (same size & shape).

E.g. Chlamydomonas

Anisogamy

Morphologically dissimilar gametes ka fusion (different size & shape).

E.g. Some algae, fungi

Oogamy

Anisogamy ka type — large, non-motile egg + small, motile sperm.

E.g. Humans

Sexual Reproduction Flow: Male Gamete (Sperm) + Female Gamete (Ovum/Egg) → Zygote

4. Binary Fission & Multiple Fission

Binary Fission

  • Unicellular organism 2 equal, genetically identical daughter cells mein divide hota hai.
  • Sirf unicellular organisms mein common.
E.g. Amoeba, Leishmania

Multiple Fission

  • Nucleus multiple times divide hota hai, cytoplasm baad mein → many daughter cells.
  • Many offsprings produced.
E.g. Plasmodium (malarial parasite)

5. Fragmentation & Budding

Fragmentation

  • Body pieces mein break hoti hai → har fragment ek naya organism banata hai.
  • Simple multicellular organisms mein hota hai.
E.g. Spirogyra (green algae), Rhodophyte (red algae), Sea anemone

Budding

  • Parent organism pe bud ya outgrowth se naya organism develop hota hai.
  • Cell division se chhota bud banta hai → grow karke detach hota hai.
  • Simple multicellular + unicellular dono mein hota hai.
E.g. Hydra (multicellular), Yeast (unicellular)

7. Vegetative Propagation

Kya hota hai?

  • Naya plant parent plant ke vegetative parts (root, stem, leaf) se grow karta hai.
  • Cutting ya grafting se bhi ho sakta hai.
  • Seeds involve nahi hote — naya plant genetically identical to parent.

📌 Potato ke "Eyes"

Potato ke buds ko "eyes" kehte hain — ye axillary buds hote hain stem ke nodes pe. Ye new potato plant mein grow ho sakte hain.

Types of Vegetative Propagation

  1. Cutting: Plant ka part (stem/leaf) cut karke soil mein plant karo. Rose, Money Plant, Banana, Sugarcane
  2. Layering: Stem ko bend karke soil se cover karo. Lemon, Strawberry
  3. Grafting: Doosre plant ki cutting ko plant ke stem se attach karo. Rose

8. Tissue Culture

Kya hai?

  • Modern method of asexual reproduction — laboratory mein sterile conditions mein cells/tissues se naye plants grow kiye jaate hain.
  • E.g. Ornamental plants like snake plant mein widely used.
  • Aise plants ki cost bahut high hoti hai.

11. Types of Flowers & Plants

Bisexual Flowers (Hermaphrodite)

  • Same flower mein dono male (stamens) + female (pistil) organs.
  • Self-pollination + Cross-pollination dono capable.
  • Hibiscus, Mustard, Lily, Sunflower

Unisexual Flowers

  • Sirf ek organ — ya stamens ya pistil.
  • Only cross-pollination capable.
  • Male (stamens only): Corn, Cucumber
  • Female (carpels only): Papaya, Watermelon

Monoecious Plants

Male + female reproductive parts same plant pe.

Maize, Cucumber

Dioecious Plants

Male + female reproductive parts separate plants pe.

Papaya, Date Palm

12. Pollination

Pollination kya hai?

Pollen (plant ke male reproductive cells) ka transfer anther se stigma (female part) tak → fertilization → seed development.

1. Self-Pollination (Autogamy)

  • Same flower ya same plant ke doosre flower ke stigma pe pollen.
  • No genetic variation.
  • Pea, Tomato

2. Cross-Pollination (Allogamy)

  • Ek plant ke anther se different plant ke same species ke stigma pe pollen.
  • Genetic diversity promote karta hai.
  • Apple, Sunflower

13. Fruit-Related Specific Terms

False Fruit

  • Ovary ke alawa flower ke doosre parts se develop hota hai.
  • Fertilization zaruri nahi — thallus bhi involved.
  • Apple, Cashew nut

Drupe Fruit

  • Hard shell + seed + inner flesh wala fruit.
  • Coconut

Parthenocarpic Fruits

  • Seedless fruits — ovule ke fertilization ke bina develop.
  • Banana, Grape, Cucumber

14. Human Reproductive System

🔵 Male Reproductive Parts

  • Testes: Scrotum mein — sperm + testosterone produce. Sperm production ~age 10-12 yrs.
  • Vas Deferens (Sperm Duct): Mature sperm ko epididymis se urethra tak le jaata hai.
  • Seminal Vesicles + Prostate Gland: Seminal fluid add → Semen banta hai (nutrients + medium for sperm).
  • Urethra: Semen aur urine dono carry karta hai — kabhi ek saath nahi.

🔴 Female Reproductive Parts

  • Fallopian Tubes (Oviducts): Sperm + egg meet → fertilization hota hai. Cilia egg ko uterus ki taraf push karte hain.
  • Uterus (Womb): Fertilized egg implant hota hai → foetus develop hota hai. Innermost lining = Endometrium.
  • Cervix: Uterus ka lower part → vagina se connect. Childbirth mein open hona zaroori.
📌 Important Facts
  • Cervical Cancer: Human Papillomavirus (HPV) se hota hai.
  • Uterine wall pregnancy ke liye thick hoti hai → agar pregnancy nahi hoti → lining shed → Menstruation.

15. Menopause & Placenta

Menopause

  • Women mein menstruation ka permanent cessation.
  • Reproductive years ka ant.
  • Late 40s to early 50s — average age 51.
  • Ovaries eggs release karna band → Estrogen + Progesterone decrease.

Placenta

  • Mother aur baby ke beech link.
  • Umbilical cord = placenta se — substances carry karta hai foetus se/tak.
  • Umbilical cord mein 3 blood vessels:
    • 1 Umbilical Vein: O₂ + nutrients → Mother to Foetus.
    • 2 Umbilical Arteries: CO₂ + waste → Foetus to Mother.

16. Fertilization

Fertilization: Male gamete (sperm) + Female gamete (egg) = Zygote

Internal Fertilisation

  • Female body ke andar fusion.

17. Process of Fertilization (Step-by-Step)

1

Gametogenesis

Gametes banane ka process — males mein sperm, females mein eggs.

2

Insemination

Sperm ka female reproductive tract mein transfer (intercourse ke doran).

3

Fertilisation

Sperm + Egg fuse → Zygote (new baby ki pehli cell).

4

Implantation

Zygote → Blastocyst develop → Uterine wall se attach → Pregnancy start.

5

Gestation

Embryo grow karta hai → Foetus banta hai uterus ke andar.

6

Parturition

Final stage — baby delivered through childbirth.

18. IVF, Totipotency & Parthenogenesis

🔬 In-Vitro Fertilisation (IVF)

Egg + Sperm ko mother ke body ke bahar laboratory mein combine kiya jaata hai.

"Test tube baby" method.

🌱 Totipotency

Single cell ki ability ki ek fertile, adult individual produce kar sake.

Tissue culture ka basis.

🐝 Parthenogenesis

Asexual reproduction — organism unfertilised egg se develop hota hai, sperm ki zarurat nahi.

Honey bees, Lizards
