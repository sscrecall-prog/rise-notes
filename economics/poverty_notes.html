Poverty – Fundamentals

Poverty Kya Hota Hai?

Definition: Poverty ek aisi condition hai jisme koi individual ke paas basic standard of living ke liye zaruri financial resources aur essentials nahi hote.

Ye sirf paison ki baat nahi — environmental, legal, social, economic, aur political causes bhi hote hain.

🏠 Real Life Example

Ramesh ek kisan hai Bihar me: Uske paas na khana hai din mein ek baar, na saaf paani, na doctor ke paas jaane ke paise, na bachon ki school fees. Ye hai poverty — sirf "paisa nahi hai" nahi, balkia ek complete deprivation ki state hai.

2

Types of Poverty

① Absolute Poverty (Nirpeksh Garibi) 🍚

Jab log basic necessities se bilkul mahroom hote hain — khana, saaf paani, ghar, kapde, healthcare, education.

💡 Example

Sub-Saharan Africa ke bahut se log jo din me ek waqt ka khana bhi nahi khaa paate — ye hai Absolute Poverty. India mein bhi 1990s tak kaafi log iss category mein the. Ye internationally fixed threshold hai ($2.15/day World Bank ke hisaab se).

② Relative Poverty (Sapekhsh Garibi) ⚖️

Jab kisi ka income ya resources uski society ke average se significantly kam ho. Ye comparative hai.

💡 Example

Delhi mein ek autowala ₹20,000 mahine kamaata hai — khana, ghar sab hai. Lekin uske neighbourhood ke logon ki average income ₹80,000 hai. Compare karke wo relatively garib hai. USA mein bhi relative poverty exist karta hai even though absolute poverty rare hai.

③ Subjective Poverty (Vyaktigat Garibi) 🧠

Personal perception pe based — log feel karte hain ki poor hain, chahe unki actual income thik ho. Society comparison, expectations, psychology sab matter karta hai.

💡 Example

Priya ek software engineer hai ₹70,000/month income ke saath. Lekin uske friends IIT grads hain jo ₹3L kamaate hain. Social media pe unka lifestyle dekhke Priya subjectively poor feel karti hai — even though wo actually poor nahi hai. Ye psychological poverty hai!

😔
Usually Poor

Hamesha poverty line ke neeche. Chronic poverty. Example: Daily wage laborers jo kaam na mile toh bhookhe rehte hain.

🔄
Churning Poor

Poverty line ke upar-neeche aate jaate hain. Example: Seasonal workers — fasal ke time paise hain, baaki time nahi.

🤞
Occasionally Poor

Mostly line ke upar, kabhi kabhi neeche gir jaate hain. Example: Small shopkeeper jiska ek saal mein business fail ho jaata hai.

3

Poverty Estimation in India

Method: India me poverty measure karte hain kisi ke income ya consumption level dekhke. Agar ye set minimum se kam hai → wo Below Poverty Line (BPL) hai.

Who does it: NITI Aayog ka task force → data NSSO (National Sample Survey Office) se liya jaata hai → MoSPI ke under.

🛒 BPL Ka Matlab Kya?

Agar government ne decide kiya ki ek insaan ko minimum ₹1,000/month chahiye survive karne ke liye — aur Suresh ₹800 mein guzaara karta hai — toh Suresh BPL (Below Poverty Line) hai. Usse government schemes ka fayda milega (ration card, PM Awas Yojana, etc.)

4

Pre-Independence Estimation

📚 Dadabhai Naoroji – "Poverty and Un-British Rule in India"
  • India mein poverty line ki sabse pehli estimation Dadabhai Naoroji ne ki.
  • Estimated: ₹16 to ₹35 per capita per year
  • India ke National Income ka bhi pehla estimation inhi ne diya.
  • Used concept of "Prisoner's Menu" — jail me jo basic khana milta hai (flour, rice, dal) — usi ki cost se subsistence calculate kiya.
🧠 Prisoner's Menu Trick

Sochte the: "Ek insaan ko survive karne ke liye at least jail jaisa khana chahiye. Jail mein jo khana milta hai uski cost = minimum poverty line." Kitna brilliant aur practical approach tha!

🏛️ National Planning Committee (1938)
  • Set up by Subhash Chandra Bose under chairmanship of JL Nehru.
  • Poverty line estimated: ₹15 to ₹20 per capita per month
  • Based on minimum standard of living with nutritional requirements included.
🏙️ The Bombay Plan (1944)
  • Set of proposals by influential business leaders of Bombay for India's development.
  • Suggested poverty line: ₹75 per capita per year
5

Post-Independence – Poverty Committees

1971
VM Dandekar & N Rath
  • Pehla systematic assessment of poverty in India (NSSO data based).
  • Expenditure-based poverty line.
  • 2250 calories per day — rural aur urban dono ke liye.
🍽️ Example

Unhone socha: "Ek insaan ko din mein minimum 2250 calories chahiye survive karne ke liye. Kitne mein wo calories milti hain — wohi poverty line." Simple aur direct approach!

1979
Alagh Committee (Chairman: YK Alagh)
  • Rural aur urban ke liye alag alag calorie requirements define kiye.
  • Rural: 2400 calories → ₹49.09/month
  • Urban: 2100 calories → ₹56.64/month
🌾 Why Rural = More Calories?

Gaon me log physically zyada kaam karte hain — khet me jotna, bhoj uthana. Isliye rural workers ko zyada calories chahiye (2400 vs 2100 urban). Bahut logical!

1993
Lakdawala Committee
  • Poverty line defined on basis of household per capita consumption expenditure.
  • Used CPI-IW (Consumer Price Index – Industrial Workers) and CPI-AL (for Agricultural Labourers).
  • Suggested: State specific poverty lines banayein (ek size fit all nahi).
  • Calorie-based calculation continue kiya.
💡 State-Specific Lines Kyun?

Mumbai me ₹1,000 mein sirf ek week ka ration aata hai. Lekin Chhattisgarh ke ek gaon mein ₹1,000 se poora mahina nikal sakta hai. Isliye ek hi poverty line sab jagah apply nahi hoti!

2005 (report 2009)
Tendulkar Committee ⭐ (Most Important)
  • Set up to address shortcomings of previous methods.
  • Poverty line based on Purchasing Power Parity = ₹33 per day
  • Shifted away from calorie-only model — broader approach.
  • Added private expenditure on health & education.
  • Replaced URP (Uniform Reference Period) with MRP (Mixed Reference Period):
30 Days Recall

Food aur frequent items ke liye (doodh, sabzi, etc.)

365 Days Recall

Infrequent non-food items ke liye (kapde, footwear, durables)

Poverty Line (2011-12):
🌾 Rural: ₹816 per capita per month
🏙️ Urban: ₹1,000 per capita per month
📊 Overall poverty: 21.9% | Rural: 25.7% | Urban: 13.7%
⚠️ ₹33/Day Controversy

Tendulkar Committee ne ₹33/day urban poverty line set ki. Log bolne lage: "Delhi mein ₹33 mein kya hoga? Ek chai bhi ₹20 ki hai!" Bahut criticism aayi ki ye line bahut neechi hai. Isliye Rangarajan Committee bani.

2012 (report 2014)
Rangarajan Committee
  • Poverty basket mein 3 components: food items + essential non-food + behaviorally determined non-food items.
  • Calories ke saath protein aur fat bhi include kiya.
  • Introduced MMRP (Modified Mixed Reference Period) — weekly, monthly, yearly basis pe compute kiya.
Poverty Line (Recommended):
🌾 Rural: ₹972 per capita per month
🏙️ Urban: ₹1,407 per capita per month
📦 "Behaviorally Determined" Kya Hota Hai?

Kuch cheezein technically zaruri nahi hain par society mein rehne ke liye practically important hain — jaise mobile recharge, school uniform, bus fare. Rangarajan ne kaha: inhe bhi poverty basket mein shamil karo!

MPI – Multidimensional Poverty Index & HDI

Global MPI – Kya Hai?
  • Introduced 2010 by UNDP + OPHI (Oxford Poverty & Human Development Initiative).
  • Uses 10 indicators across 3 dimensions: Health, Education, Standard of Living.
🏥 Health (2 indicators)
  • Nutrition
  • Child Mortality
📚 Education (2 indicators)
  • Years in schooling
  • School attendance
🏠 Standard of Living (6)
  • Cooking fuel, Sanitation
  • Drinking water, Electricity
  • Housing, Assets
💡 MPI vs Income Poverty

Ek gaon mein sab log ₹2,000/month kamate hain (above income poverty line). Lekin unke ghar mein na bijli hai, na toilet, na clean water. Income ke hisaab se poor nahi — lekin MPI ke hisaab se definitely multidimensionally poor hain! MPI zyada comprehensive hai.

National MPI (India) – NITI Aayog
  • Launched November 2021 by NITI Aayog.
  • Uses 12 indicators (vs 10 in global MPI).
  • Data source: NFHS (National Family Health Survey)
  • Multidimensional poverty declined since 2005-06.
  • 24.82 crore people escaped multidimensional poverty in last 9 years.
  • Uttar Pradesh: Largest number lifted out of poverty (absolute numbers).
Highest MPI (Most Poor)

🔴 Bihar

Lowest MPI (Least Poor)

Human Development Index (HDI)
  • Created in 1990 by Mahbub ul Haq (Pakistani Economist) + Amartya Sen (Indian Economist).
  • Amartya Sen: First Indian economist to win Nobel Prize in Economics (1998).
💊 Health

Indicator: Life expectancy at birth

📖 Knowledge

Indicators: Expected years + Mean years of schooling

💰 Standard of Living

Indicator: GNI per capita (PPP $)

🌟 HDI Why Important?

Pehle countries ki progress sirf GDP se napta tha. Mahbub ul Haq aur Amartya Sen ne kaha: "GDP se sirf income pata chalti hai — logon ki life quality nahi!" Isliye HDI banaya — ek holistic measure jo health + education + income sab dekhta hai. Ab UNDP har saal Human Development Report publish karta hai.

HCR, EPI & MVI

📊 Head Count Ratio (HCR)

Also known as: Poverty Headcount Index

Population ka wo percentage jo BPL mein jeeta hai.

Formula
HCR = (No. of Multidimensionally Poor People) ÷ (Total Population)
Example: Agar 100 mein se 22 log BPL hain → HCR = 22%
🧮 Simple Example

Ek village mein 500 log hain. 110 BPL hain. HCR = 110/500 = 22%. Matlab us village mein har 5 mein se 1 se zyada garib hai.

🚢 Export Preparedness Index (EPI)
  • Publisher: NITI Aayog
  • Structure: 4 Pillars — Policy (20%), Business Ecosystem (40%), Export Ecosystem (20%), Export Performance (20%)
  • Measures states' readiness to export goods.
🌍 Multidimensional Vulnerability Index (MVI)
  • Publisher: UNGA (United Nations General Assembly)
  • Measures structural vulnerability and lack of resilience across 3 dimensions:
  • Economic Environmental Social
  • Focus: Specially for Small Island Developing States (SIDS) aur vulnerable nations.
🌏 MVI ka Use

Bangladesh floods se vulnerable hai. Maldives sea level rise se. Aise deshon ki vulnerability naapne ke liye MVI banaya gaya. India jaisa bada desh kam vulnerable — chota island nation zyada.

MPC – Marginal Propensity to Consume & Multiplier

MPC Kya Hota Hai?

Definition: Jab kisi ki income badhti hai toh usme se jo additional income ka consumption pe kharch hota hai — uska proportion MPC hai.

Formula
MPC = ΔC / ΔY
ΔC = Change in Consumption  |  ΔY = Change in Income  |  MPC value: 0 to 1
Example: Income ₹10,000 badhi → Spending ₹1,000 badha → MPC = 1000/10000 = 0.1
Matlab: Har extra ₹1 mein se sirf 10 paise kharch kiye, 90 paise bachaye.
👨‍💼 Real Life MPC Example

Ramu (poor labourer): ₹1,000 bonus mila → poore ₹1,000 kharch kar diye! MPC = 1.0 (near to 1)
Shyam (rich businessman): ₹1,00,000 bonus mila → sirf ₹10,000 kharch kiye, baaki invest kiya. MPC = 0.1

Conclusion: Garib logon ka MPC zyada hota hai (unhe survive karne ke liye sab kharchna padta hai). Isliye poor logon ko paisa dena economy mein zyada circulation laata hai!

Autonomous Expenditure & Multiplier
  • Autonomous Expenditure: Wo spending jo income level ke chahe kuch bhi ho — hoti rehti hai (income pe dependent nahi).
  • Example: Government infrastructure spending — recession mein bhi hogi.
C = A + cY
A = Autonomous (income-independent) consumption
cY = Income-dependent consumption
c = MPC  |  Y = Income level
Savings = 1 − c
Multiplier = 1 / (1 − c)
📐 Multiplier Example

Agar MPC = 0.8, toh Multiplier = 1/(1-0.8) = 1/0.2 = 5
Matlab: Government ₹100 crore invest kare roads pe → economy mein ₹500 crore ka effect hoga! (Logon ko wages mile → wo kharchein → dukan wale kamayein → wo kharchein...) Yahi hai multiplier effect ka jadoo!

Special Economic Zones (SEZ)

SEZ ke 4 Goals:

① Exports promote karna  |  ② Foreign investment attract karna  |  ③ Employment generate karna  |  ④ Foreign exchange earn karna

📅 Key Dates
  • SEZ Policy Announced: 2000
  • SEZ Act Enacted: 2005
  • Asia's first Export Processing Zone (EPZ): Kandla, Gujarat (1965)
🏭 SEZ Kya Hota Hai?

Ek specially designated area jahan different rules apply hote hain — tax benefits, customs duty waiver, easier regulations. Example: Noida SEZ mein garment factories ko export pe custom duty nahi lagta. Companies yahan aa ke produce karti hain, duniya mein bechti hain. Win-win!

⚠️ Challenge 1: MAT

Minimum Alternate Tax — SEZ developers pe bhi lagaaya gaya, unka attraction kam hua.

💸 Impact

Companies bolne lagi: "Pehle tax-free tha, ab MAT bhi lag raha hai — toh SEZ mein kyun aayein?"

⚠️ Challenge 2: Sunset Clause

Tax holidays (Section 10AA) sirf un units ko jo before April 1, 2020 shuru hue. Baad wali units ko direct tax benefit nahi.

🌅 Sunset Clause Kya?

Jaise "sun set ho gaya" — ek time baad benefits khatam ho jaate hain. 2020 ke baad SEZ setup karne wali companies ko tax holiday nahi mila.

National Infrastructure Pipeline (NIP)
  • Government initiative for world-class infrastructure + quality of life improvement.
  • Duration: 2020–2025 (5-year plan)
  • Total Investment: ₹111 Lakh Crore (₹111 Trillion!)
  • Funding: Centre 39% + States 39% + Private 22%
  • Top sectors: Energy (₹25L Cr), Roads (₹20L Cr), Irrigation/Rural (₹16L Cr)
🏗️ NIP ka Poverty Connection

Acchi roads → goods easily milein → prices kam → garibon ko faida. Bijli → industries → jobs. Clean water projects → health improve. Infrastructure development = Direct poverty reduction ka tool!

Inequality Measurement – Lorenz Curve & Gini Coefficient

Lorenz Curve 📈
  • Ek graphical representation hai income inequality ya wealth distribution ka.
  • Line of Equality (45° diagonal): Perfect equality — har insaan ki equal income.
  • Lorenz Curve is line ke neeche hoti hai.
  • Curve jitni zyada line se dur hogi — inequality utni zyada hogi.
Gini Coefficient 🔢
  • Lorenz Curve se derived — inequality ko ek number deta hai.
Formula
Gini = A / (A + B)
A = Area between Line of Equality and Lorenz Curve  |  B = Area below Lorenz Curve

Gini = 0 ✅

Perfect Equality — har insaan ki same income. Theoretical ideal. Koi bhi country achieve nahi karti.

Gini = 1 ❌

Perfect Inequality — ek insaan ke paas sab kuch, baaki sab ke paas kuch nahi. Extreme case.

🌍 Real Life Gini Coefficients

🇳🇴 Norway: ~0.25 (bahut equal) — strong social welfare
🇮🇳 India: ~0.35 (moderate inequality)
🇧🇷 Brazil: ~0.53 (bahut unequal) — historic inequality
🇿🇦 South Africa: ~0.63 (world's most unequal!) — apartheid ka asar

Gini jitna zyada → inequality utni zyada → garib aur garib, amir aur amir!
