Evolution of Panchayat

Balwant Rai Mehta Committee, 1957

Recommendation: 3-tier Panchayati Raj System.

  1. Gram Panchayat — Village level
  2. Panchayat Samiti — Block level
  3. Zila Parishad — District level

Implementation

Rajasthan: 1st State to implement Panchayati Raj at Nagaur on 2 October 1959.

Followed by Andhra Pradesh on 11 October 1959.

Ashok Mehta Committee, 1977

Proposed 2-tier Panchayati Raj:

  1. Zila Parishad — main unit
  2. Mandal Panchayat

G.V.K. Rao Committee, 1985

  • Constitution ke working ko Panchayats and Municipalities ke relation me reviewed.
  • Zila Parishad ko development ke liye nodal agency propose kiya.

L. M. Singhvi Committee, 1986

  • PRIs ko constitutional status recommend karne wali first committee — source.
  • Panchayats ki financial autonomy advocated.
  • Gram Sabha ko direct democracy ka embodiment kaha.
  • Cluster of villages ke liye Nyaya Panchayats recommend.

Thungon Committee, 1988 + Gadgil Committee, 1988

  • Rural development me PRIs ka role examine.
  • All three levels ke members directly elected.
  • Term fixed at 5 years.
  • Reservation for SC, ST and Women.

73rd Constitutional Amendment Act, 1992

  • Panchayati Raj institutions establish karna mandatory banaya.
  • Part IX, Articles 243–243O inserted.
  • 11th Schedule added containing 29 functional items.
  • Enforced on 24 April 1993; source notes this is celebrated as Panchayati Raj Day.
  • Then PM: P. V. Narasimha Rao.

Articles 243–243E — Panchayats

Article 243 — Definition

Gram Sabha: Village-level Panchayat area se related electoral rolls me registered persons ka body.

Article 243A — Gram Sabha

State Legislature by law village level par Gram Sabha ko powers/functions provide kar sakti hai.

Article 243B — Constitution of Panchayats

  • Every State me village, intermediate and district levels par Panchayats.
  • 3-tier system.
  • Intermediate-level Panchayat population 20 lakhs se exceed na karne wale State me constitute karna necessary nahi — source wording.

Three Levels

  • Village → Gram Panchayat
  • Block → Panchayat Samiti
  • District → Zila Parishad

Page diagram also lists village-level terms: Gram Sabha, Gram Panchayat, Nyaya Panchayat.

Article 243C — Composition

  • All Panchayat seats direct election se filled.
  • Village-level Chairperson ka election State law ke according.
  • Intermediate and district-level Chairperson indirect election se.

Article 243D — Reservation

  • SC/ST seats population proportion me reserved.
  • 1/3 seats women ke liye reserved.

Article 243E — Duration

  • Term: 5 years, unless earlier dissolved.
  • Election 5-year term end hone se pehle.
  • Early dissolution: election within 6 months; remaining term less than 6 months ho to election mandatory nahi — source.
  • Reconstituted Panchayat only original term ka remainder serve karegi.

Articles 243F–243M

243F — Disqualifications

  • State Legislature-made law disqualification cause kar sakta hai.
  • Certain offence conviction/insolvency state election law ke according.
  • Disqualification prescribed authority decide karti hai.
  • Source: minimum required age 21 years; less than 25 hone solely par disqualify nahi.
Source side-note: High Court deals with election disputes of MP/MLAs.

243G — Powers & Responsibilities

State Legislature Panchayats ko powers/authority de sakti hai to function as institutions of self-government.

243H — Taxes

State Legislature Panchayats ko taxes, duties, tolls and fees levy/collect/appropriate karne aur grants-in-aid ke provisions authorize kar sakti hai.

243I — State Finance Commission

Governor Finance Commission constitute karta hai to financial position review; recommendations State Legislature ke before laid.

243J — Audit

Panchayat accounts ka audit; source emphasis financial accountability, transparency and responsible management.

243K — Elections

  • Panchayat elections State Election Commission conducts.
  • State Election Commissioner Governor appoints.
  • Service conditions/tenure Governor determines.
  • Removal same manner as High Court judge — source.

243L — Union Territories

Part IX provisions UTs par certain modifications/exceptions ke saath apply.

243M — Certain Areas

Tribal regions aur some North-Eastern areas ko unique governance/traditions ke respect me exclusion.

Nothing in this Part applies to: Nagaland, Meghalaya, Mizoram; hill areas of Manipur — source.

PESA Act, 1996 & Articles 243N–243O

PESA Act, 1996

Panchayats (Extension to the Scheduled Areas) Act, 1996 Part IX provisions ko Scheduled Areas — Fifth Schedule Areas — tak extend karta hai.

Source Fact: Delhi is the only UT where provisions of Panchayats are not applicable.

243N — Continuance of Existing Laws & Panchayats

New Panchayati Raj system me smooth transition ke liye existing laws temporarily valid rahte hain while State necessary changes karta hai.

243O — Bar to Interference by Courts

Courts Panchayat constituencies define karne ya seats assign karne ki process me interfere nahi kar sakte — source.

Municipalities — Urban Local Government

  • Introduced through 74th Constitutional Amendment, 1992.
  • Part IXA, Articles 243P–243ZG.
  • 12th Schedule added with 18 functional items.

Historical Facts

  • First Municipal Corporation: Madras, 1687–1688.
  • 1870: Lord Mayo's resolution of Financial Decentralisation.
  • Ripon's resolution, 1882: “Magna Carta of Local Self-Government” — source.
  • Municipal Corporations in Calcutta & Bombay set up in 1726.
  • Ripon known as “Father of Local Self-Government”.

8 Types of Urban Local Government

  1. Municipal Corporation
  2. Municipality
  3. Notified Area Committee
  4. Cantonment Board (Military)
  5. Township
  6. Town Area Committee
  7. Port Trust
  8. Special Purpose Agency

3 Types of Municipalities

  1. Nagar Panchayat: Transitional Area — example Kalyanpur.
  2. Municipal Council: Small urban area — example Chhindwara.
  3. Municipal Corporation: Larger urban area — example Indore.

All seats direct elections; elections by State Election Commission.

Part IXB — Co-operative Societies

  • Source heading: Articles 243ZH to 243ZT.
  • Inserted by 97th Constitutional Amendment Act, 2011.
  • Grants constitutional status and protection to co-operative societies.
  • Provisions relate to formation, regulation and functioning of Co-operative societies.
