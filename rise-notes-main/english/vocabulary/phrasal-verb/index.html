🎯 Special Table: "The Distribution Quad" (सामान बांटने वाली Verbs)

Ye 4 verbs "to distribute / बाँटना" jaisa meaning dete hain, par har ek ka use alag context mein hota hai:

Phrasal VerbContext (Kab Use Karein)
Give outGeneral distribution — leaflets, news bantna
Hand outGroup ko cheezein dena — test papers, snacks
Deal outShares ya punishments dena (zyada formal)
Hand downPurani generation se nayi generation ko info/wealth pass karna

🎯 Special Table: Verbs of "Ending" or "Stopping" (Cloze Test mein confuse hote hain)

VerbNuance (Fark)

🎯 Common Confusions (Quick Tips)

Verb PhraseCommon ConfusionQuick Tip
Agree with vs Agree toPerson vs ProposalUse with for people/opinions; to for plans.
Call at vs Call onPlace vs PersonUse at for locations; on for individuals.
Back off vs Back downDistance vs ArgumentOff is moving away; Down is giving up a fight.
